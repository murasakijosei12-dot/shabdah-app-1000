How to Set This in Gmail (30 Seconds)
  1. Click the green "Copy Formatted Signature" button above.
  2. Click "Open Gmail Signature Settings" (or go to Gmail ⚙️ Settings → See all settings → General → scroll to Signature).
  3. Click "+ Create new", name it Shabdah Official, and press Cmd + V (Mac) or Ctrl + V (Windows) directly into the signature box.
  4. Under Signature defaults, select Shabdah Official for both "For new emails use" and "On reply/forward use".
  5. Scroll to the very bottom of the page and click Save Changes.
