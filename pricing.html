Questions

Is this a subscription?

No. You pay once for the Founding Edition — all three directions included — and it stays yours. There is no renewal, no expiry, and no card kept on file.

How do I activate my purchase?

Log into your account on the website with the same email used at checkout. Your purchased access unlocks immediately across all your devices.

Can I get a refund?

Within 14 days, for a valid reason. Write to us from the contact page with the reason and the payment is returned. See the Terms of Use for the full wording.
